1. Acceptance of these terms

By browsing this website, sending us an enquiry or signing up for any VFI service, you agree to the terms set out on this page. If you do not agree with any part of them, please stop using the site and tell us before we begin work on your case.

Where you sign a separate written service agreement with us, that agreement takes priority over anything on this page if the two ever conflict.

2. The services we provide

VFI offers study-abroad counselling, university and course shortlisting, application support, document guidance, test-preparation referrals and visa application assistance.

We help you prepare and submit a strong application, but we are an education consultancy — we are not a university and not an immigration authority. We cannot promise admission, a scholarship, a visa or any other particular outcome, and any timelines we give you are estimates based on past cases.

3. Your responsibilities as a student

You are responsible for meeting the deadlines we agree together, attending the sessions you book, and paying any third-party fees on time. Missed deadlines are the most common reason a good application fails.

Please read every document before you sign it, including offer letters, accommodation contracts and visa forms. The final decision on which university to accept, which loan to take and whether to travel is always yours.

4. Accuracy of the information you give us

Everything you tell us — academic records, test scores, work history, financial documents and immigration history — must be true, complete and your own. We rely on what you give us when we advise you and when we apply on your behalf.

If you give us false, altered or incomplete information, we may stop work on your file immediately and without refund. You accept responsibility for any refusal, ban or other consequence that follows from information you supplied.

5. Third-party universities and partners

Universities, English test centres, insurers, accommodation providers, banks and visa authorities are independent organisations. We pass on their requirements and fees as accurately as we can.

Their admission criteria, processing times, decisions and refund rules are set by them and can change without notice. VFI is not responsible for the acts, delays or decisions of any third party, and any contract you enter into with them is between you and them.

6. Fees and payments

Our profile evaluation and counselling are free. Where a paid service applies, we will tell you the amount, what it covers and when it is due in writing before you commit to it.

Third-party costs such as application fees, test fees, visa charges and university deposits are separate and are paid by you. Accepted payment methods, invoicing and our refund rules are set out in full on our Payment Terms page.
