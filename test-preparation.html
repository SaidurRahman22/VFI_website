Inside the classroom

How the course runs

Five stages, roughly eight weeks, and a trainer who knows your weak sections by name.

01Diagnostic test

You sit a free full-length paper before you enrol. We mark it section by section and show you exactly which skills are costing you marks — no guesswork, no generic advice.

02Personalised study plan

Your trainer turns that diagnostic into a week-by-week plan with a target score, daily practice targets and a realistic test date that fits your application deadlines.

03Live classes

Small live batches, online or at a VFI centre, where strategy is taught and then practised in the same session. Every class is recorded so you can revisit anything you missed.

04Weekly mock tests

One timed, invigilated mock every week on the real interface, followed by a review session on the questions you got wrong and the ones you got right by luck.

05Final review and booking

A last one-to-one on pacing and exam-day nerves, a rapid-revision pack for the final week, and help booking the slot and centre that suits your timeline.

Before you enrol

Frequently asked questions

How long does a course take?

English proficiency courses run six to eight weeks; GRE, GMAT, SAT and ACT typically run ten to twelve. Weekend and fast-track batches compress the same syllabus if your deadline is tight.

How many students are in a batch?

Twelve at most, and usually fewer. Speaking and writing practice only works if the trainer can hear and mark everyone in the room, so we hold that cap firmly.

Are classes online or in person?

Both, and you can mix them. Every session is live rather than pre-recorded, and every recording stays in your portal for the length of the course, wherever you attended from.

What if I need to retake the test?

Tell us your score and we will build a short retake plan around the sections that let you down — including revision classes and fresh mocks at no extra course fee within six months of finishing.

Do I get study materials?

Yes. Enrolment includes the printed workbook, the online question bank, all mock tests with detailed scoring, and vocabulary and formula sheets you keep after the course ends.

Not sure which test to sit?

Take a free diagnostic with a VFI trainer and find out which exam plays to your strengths — and what score your shortlist actually requires.
