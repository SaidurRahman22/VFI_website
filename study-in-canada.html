Why study in Canada?

Co-op & internships

Pathway to PR

Eligibility Criteria for Studying in Canada

Requirements vary between institutions, but this is the baseline Canadian universities and colleges expect.

  • 12 years of schooling with strong final results
  • IELTS 6.0 – 6.5 overall (or TOEFL / PTE / Duolingo)
  • Valid passport covering your whole stay
  • Official academic transcripts and application fee
  • A recognised bachelor's degree in a relevant field
  • IELTS 6.5 overall (or TOEFL / PTE / Duolingo)
  • Statement of purpose and updated CV
  • Two to three letters of recommendation

English test scores

Statement of purpose

Letters of recommendation

Proof of financial support

Bank statements, GIC or a sanctioned education loan.

Study Permit for Canada

Courses longer than six months need a study permit. You'll also submit biometrics, and most students buy a GIC as proof of first-year living funds.

CAD 235Study permit application fee
CAD 85Biometrics fee, per applicant
GICProof of funds for your first year

Documents required for your study permit

  • Valid passport
  • Letter of Acceptance (LOA)
  • Proof of financial support / GIC
  • Provincial Attestation Letter, where required
  • Biometrics submitted at a VAC
  • Medical exam, if requested (CAD 150 – 300)
  • Statement of purpose / study plan
  • Passport-size photographs

FAQs

Which intake should I apply for?

Fall (September) is the most popular and has the widest choice of courses and scholarships. Winter (January) and Summer (May) are smaller intakes with limited availability.

How many hours can I work while studying?

Up to 24 hours per week off campus during term, and full time during scheduled breaks.

How far in advance should I apply?

Start 8–12 months before your intended intake, so there's time for offers, GIC and the study permit.

What does the study permit cost?

CAD 235 for the permit plus CAD 85 for biometrics. A medical exam, if requested, costs roughly CAD 150–300.

Do I need GRE or GMAT?

Not for most programs. GMAT is common for MBA admission and GRE for a few specialised master's courses.

How long is the post-study work permit?

Up to 3 years, depending on the length of your program — and that Canadian work experience counts towards permanent residency.
