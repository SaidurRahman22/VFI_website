What to expect

Our hiring process

Five steps, roughly two to three weeks, and a reply from a real person at every stage.

01Apply

Send your CV and a short note about the role that caught your eye. No cover-letter essays required — tell us what you have done and what you want to do next.

02Screening call

A 20-minute call with our talent team to walk through your background, answer your questions and be upfront about pay, hours and location from the start.

03Team interview

A conversation with the hiring manager and one teammate about how you work, how you handle pressure during peak intake, and what good looks like in the role.

04Practical assessment

A short, paid-for-your-time task drawn from real work — a mock counselling session, a sample application review or a small build, depending on the role.

05Offer

References, a written offer with the full package spelled out, and a start date that respects your notice period. Onboarding is planned before day one.
