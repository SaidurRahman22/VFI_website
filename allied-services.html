Your pre-departure checklist

Print it, tick it, travel calmly. We go through the same list with you on a call before you fly — and again the week of departure.

  • Passport and stamped visa, plus photocopies kept separately
  • Offer letter and every tuition fee receipt
  • Forex cash for week one and a loaded travel card
  • Insurance policy number and the 24-hour claims line
  • Accommodation confirmation with the exact arrival address
  • Flight tickets and your airline's baggage allowance in writing
  • Academic transcripts, certificates and test score reports
  • Essential medicines with a doctor's prescription in English
How it works

Four steps, and none of them are yours to chase

You tell us what you need. We do the comparing, the booking and the following up.

01Tell us what you need

A short call once your offer is in hand. We note your city, intake date, budget and which of these services you want us to take on.

02We compare options and quotes

You get a written shortlist for each service — rates, cover, distance and contract terms laid out side by side, with our recommendation and the reasoning behind it.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is there an extra charge for allied services?

Coordinating them is part of the support you already have with VFI. You pay the provider directly — the insurer, the airline, the housing operator — and we show you that price before you commit.

Can I use my own provider instead?

Of course. Send us what you have arranged and we will check it against your university's and your visa's requirements, so nothing gets rejected at the counter.

How early should I book accommodation?

Start shortlisting two to three months before your intake and confirm as soon as your visa is granted. In popular university cities the affordable rooms close first, often before results are even out.

What does airport pickup include?

A meet-and-greet at arrivals, luggage space matched to what you are carrying, a direct transfer to your address and your driver's contact details shared a day ahead. Delays are tracked, so a late flight does not mean a missed ride.
