Quick actions

Everything you publish here shows up on the website straight away.

How this works

  • No login — this panel is meant to run on your own machine or a private URL.
  • Content is saved in this browser. Use Backup to export a file and restore it on another computer.
  • Images are resized automatically before saving, so the site stays fast.
  • Use Pages On/Off to hide a page from the menus without deleting it.
Drop photos here or click to choose JPG or PNG — you can select several at once

Home page images

These are the fixed picture slots on the home page — the hero visual, the four round photos in the coloured “Services & Offerings” bands, and the three cards in “Multi Country Advantage”. Leave a slot empty to keep the illustrated placeholder.

Choose a country page

Edit the text, photos and lists on each destination page. Anything you leave blank keeps whatever is already on the page.

Choose a region page

Edit the Europe and Asia hub pages — the hero text and every country block (name, description, quick facts and three photos).

Student Services page

Edit the hero text and the nine service blocks on services.html — the page that holds Counselling, Applications & Admission and Visa Processing. Leave anything blank to keep what is already on the page.

Service blocks

Each block is one row on the page. Anchor is the link id used by the menu — keep counselling, applications and visa if you want those menu links to keep working. Photos look best around 800 × 680 px.

Show or hide pages

Switch a page off and it disappears from the menus and footer, and anyone opening its address sees a short “not available” notice instead. The home page cannot be switched off.
