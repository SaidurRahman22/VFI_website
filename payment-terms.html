Payment Terms

What our services cost, which fees go to other organisations, how you can pay us, and when money comes back to you.

1. What our services cost, and what is free

Profile evaluation, course counselling, university shortlisting and our standard application support are free to students.

Where a service does carry a fee — for example premium or expedited support — we will tell you the exact amount, what it covers and when it is due, in writing, before you commit. You will never be charged for something you did not agree to in advance.

2. Third-party fees you pay directly

Most of what a study-abroad journey costs goes to other organisations, not to VFI: university application fees, English test fees such as IELTS or PTE, credential evaluations, visa and biometric charges, health cover, and university tuition deposits.

These amounts are set by those organisations and can change without notice. We will give you our best estimate up front, but the amount you owe is always the one quoted by the university, test centre or government at the time you pay it.

3. Accepted payment methods

Where a fee is due to VFI, we accept bank transfer to our named company account, plus the card and mobile financial services listed on your invoice.

Please pay only into an account named on an official VFI invoice. We will never ask you to send money to a staff member's personal account or wallet — if anyone asks you to do that, stop and email dhaka@vfi-edu.com straight away.

4. Invoicing and receipts

Every payment to VFI is invoiced before it falls due and receipted once it clears. The invoice states what the fee covers, the amount, the due date and the account to pay into.

Keep your receipts — universities and visa authorities sometimes ask for proof that a fee was paid. If a receipt has not reached you within a few working days of paying, tell us so we can trace it.

5. Refunds and cancellations

You may cancel a paid VFI service at any time by writing to us. If you cancel before we have started the work, we refund what you paid in full; if the work has already begun, we refund the portion that has not yet been delivered.

Third-party fees — application, test, visa charges and university deposits — are outside our control and are refundable only under that organisation's own rules, which are often strict or non-refundable. We do not refund our service fees where an application fails because information you gave us was false or incomplete.

6. Currency and exchange rates

Our fees are quoted in Bangladeshi Taka unless your invoice says otherwise. Third-party fees are usually quoted in the currency of the destination country.

If you pay in another currency, your bank or card issuer sets the exchange rate and may add its own conversion charge, so the amount leaving your account can differ from the invoice total. Any shortfall caused by conversion or bank charges remains payable, and we will tell you if one arises.

7. Chargebacks and disputed payments

If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us first. Most disputes turn out to be a duplicate payment or a fee that went to a third party, and we can usually sort them out quickly.

Raising a chargeback with your bank before speaking to us may pause work on your application while the claim is investigated. Where a chargeback is later found to be unjustified, the original amount plus any bank charge remains payable.

8. Late payment and non-payment

Applications, deposits and visa filings run on fixed deadlines, so a late payment can cost you an intake. Please pay on or before the due date on your invoice.

If a fee due to VFI is unpaid, we may pause work on your file until it is settled, and we will tell you before we do. We will not submit an application, or release a document, that depends on an unpaid third-party fee.

9. Changes to our fees

We may change our fees as our services change. The amounts that apply to you are always the ones on your current invoice or written quotation.

A fee you have already agreed to for work in progress will not change part-way through. If we plan to change the price of an ongoing service, we will give you notice, and you may cancel under the refund terms above.

10. How to contact us

For any question about an invoice, a receipt or a refund, write to us with your name, invoice number and payment date so we can trace it. We aim to answer billing queries within a few working days.
