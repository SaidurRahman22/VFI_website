Features for partners

At VFI we take pride in equipping our partners with the VFI Partner platform. A straightforward interface and a deep course database make it quick to navigate — and quick to find the right opportunity for a student.

1

A White Labelled Platform

VFI Partner is a white-labelled tech platform with deep customisation for our partners. Fold in your own logo and brand colours across the whole platform, so your students see an experience that carries your identity and strengthens your presence in your market.

2

Explore 100,000+ course options through VFI Partner's search

An intuitive search experience that lets you search across 100,000+ courses from 900+ overseas institutions in 35 countries. Twenty-five search filters narrow thousands of courses down to a relevant few, and the eligibility filter picks up only the courses a given student actually qualifies for.

3

Student self-service platform

Once a student profile exists, you can share a personal URL with the student. That link unlocks a self-service space where they complete their own profile, upload documents securely, and see their application status in real time — so they stay engaged instead of waiting on updates.

4

Apply to multiple universities with a single application

One application form, many universities, across different countries. The system removes the redundant re-keying and automates the fiddly parts of an international application, giving your counsellors their time back for the guidance that actually needs a human.

5

Pay application and tuition fees right through the platform

Process tuition and application fees on behalf of your students without leaving the platform. Payments route through established providers and the VFI digital wallet, so transfers to universities are quick and traceable, and every transaction is easy to reconcile later.

6

Ancillary and value-added services in one place

Beyond the core study-abroad services, the platform carries the rest of the journey: student accommodation, overseas education loans, forex, airport pickup and white-labelled test preparation — so a student never has to be handed off to someone you do not control.

7

Comprehensive learning resources

A library built for people who recruit students: country guides, university-specific documents, knowledge presentations, SOP samples and application forms. It is written and kept current by our country specialists, and sourced directly from partner institutions.

Frequently asked questions

What is VFI Partner?

VFI Partner is a comprehensive tech platform built to simplify the whole student-recruitment process for study-abroad agencies — search, applications, documents, payments and reporting in one place.

How can VFI Partner help study-abroad recruiters?

It removes the repetitive parts of the job. One application reaches multiple universities, student documents live in one verified place, eligibility is checked before you apply, and you can see the status of every application without emailing anyone.

Who can register as a partner?

Any registered study-abroad consultancy, education agency or entrepreneur planning to start one. Our business team will walk you through onboarding and verify a few documents before your account goes live.

Is there a cost to join?

There is no platform licence fee for approved partners. Commercial terms — including how commissions are handled — are agreed in writing during onboarding so there are no surprises later.

What are the key features of VFI Partner?

White labelling, a 100,000+ course search with 25 filters, a student self-service portal, single-form multi-university applications, in-platform fee payment, ancillary services such as accommodation and loans, and a learning-resource library.

How do I access the VFI Partner portal?

Once your registration is approved you will receive sign-in details by email. The portal runs in any modern browser, and the mobile app is available for Android and iOS.
