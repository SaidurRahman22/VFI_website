Eligibility Criteria for Studying in New Zealand

Requirements shift between universities, but this is what New Zealand institutions typically ask for.

  • Twelve years of schooling completed with 60% or better
  • IELTS 6.0 overall, no band below 5.5
  • Or TOEFL iBT 60+ / PTE 50+ / Duolingo (selected universities)
  • Valid passport and proof of financial capacity
  • A completed bachelor's degree, 60% – 65% or equivalent
  • A related field of study, with backlogs cleared
  • IELTS 6.5+ with no band below 6.0
  • SOP, CV and letters of recommendation

Student Visa for New Zealand (Fee Paying Student Visa)

Apply once your Offer of Place arrives. The application fee is NZD 850, most students also pay the NZD 100 International Visitor Levy, and Immigration New Zealand decides most cases within five to eight weeks.

NZD 850Visa application fee
NZD 20,000Proof of funds — living costs per year
NZD 600 – 900Medical & travel insurance, per year

Documents required for your visa application

  • Valid passport
  • Offer of Place from an NZQA-approved provider
  • Acceptable medical and travel insurance
  • Evidence of NZD 20,000 living funds per year
  • Tuition fee receipt or scholarship letter
  • English proficiency score card
  • Academic transcripts & certificates
  • Medical certificate, chest X-ray and police certificate, if requested

FAQs

How many hours can I work while studying?

Eligible students can work up to 25 hours a week during term time, and full-time during scheduled course breaks.

How much money do I need to show for the visa?

NZD 20,000 per year of living costs — or NZD 1,667 a month for courses under a year — on top of your tuition fees and return airfare.

How long can I stay after I graduate?

The Post Study Work Visa runs up to three years, based on what you studied. At degree level 7 and above you can work for any employer in any role.

Do I need insurance to study in New Zealand?

Yes. Acceptable medical and travel insurance covering your whole stay is a condition of enrolment and of the student visa, typically NZD 600 – 900 a year.

Do I need to clear my backlogs before applying?

Generally yes — New Zealand universities expect a completed qualification with backlogs cleared, and your institution can issue a backlog certificate for the file.

How long does the student visa take?

Most decisions arrive within five to eight weeks of a complete application, so lodge as soon as your Offer of Place and funds evidence are ready.
