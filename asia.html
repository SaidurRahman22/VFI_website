Singapore

Compact, English-speaking and impeccably organised, Singapore packs two globally elite universities and the regional headquarters of hundreds of multinationals into a single island city.

Quick facts

  • NUS and NTU both sit inside the world's top 20
  • 12-month Long Term Visit Pass to look for work after graduating
  • English is the medium of instruction throughout
  • Asia-Pacific base for global finance, tech and logistics firms
  • Consistently ranked among the world's safest cities
Know More

Malaysia

Malaysia lets you earn a British or Australian degree in Southeast Asia — several well-known universities run full branch campuses here at a fraction of the cost of studying at the home institution.

Quick facts

  • Branch campuses of Monash, Nottingham, Heriot-Watt and Reading
  • Universiti Malaya ranks inside the world's top 100
  • Programmes delivered entirely in English
  • 12-month Graduate Pass for students from eligible countries
  • Among the lowest tuition and living costs for an English-taught degree
Know More

Japan

Japan rewards students who want depth. Its research culture, laboratory funding and links to global manufacturers are hard to match anywhere else in the region.

Quick facts

  • The University of Tokyo and Kyoto University rank in the world's top 60
  • MEXT government scholarships cover tuition plus a monthly stipend
  • Designated Activities status allows up to a year of job hunting
  • Global leader in engineering, robotics and materials science
  • Part-time work of up to 28 hours a week with permission
Know More

South Korea

South Korea has turned its universities into feeders for a technology economy that exports globally, and international students sit close to that pipeline from day one.

Quick facts

  • Seoul National University, KAIST and Yonsei are in the world's top 100
  • Global Korea Scholarship covers tuition, airfare and living costs
  • D-10 job-seeking visa lets graduates stay on to find work
  • Standout strengths in engineering, IT, design and media
  • Tuition and living costs well below Western destinations
Know More

China

China's leading universities now compete with the very best in the world, and its scholarship programmes for international students are among the most generous anywhere.

Quick facts

  • Peking University and Tsinghua University rank in the world's top 20
  • Chinese Government Scholarships cover tuition, housing and a stipend
  • Expanding range of English-taught bachelor's and master's degrees
  • Tuition well below comparable Western programmes
  • Popular for medicine, engineering, business and Chinese studies
Know More

Hong Kong

Few cities put this many highly ranked universities inside one skyline, and Hong Kong follows a degree with one of the most generous stay-back arrangements in Asia.

Quick facts

  • Five universities inside the world's top 100
  • IANG gives fresh graduates a 24-month stay with no job offer needed
  • English is a medium of instruction at every public university
  • A leading global centre for finance, law and business
  • Government and university scholarships for non-local students
Know More

UAE (Dubai)

Dubai and Abu Dhabi have imported the campuses rather than the commute — dozens of international universities teach their own accredited degrees here, in English, tax-free.

Quick facts

  • Branch campuses of UK, US, Australian and French universities
  • 10-year Golden Visa for outstanding students and graduates
  • No personal income tax on salaries
  • All programmes taught and assessed in English
  • Hub location with direct flights across Asia, Europe and Africa
Know More

India

India has opened its doors to foreign universities, and the first international branch campuses are already enrolling students — a globally awarded degree without leaving the subcontinent.

Quick facts

  • UGC regulations allow foreign universities to open Indian campuses
  • Deakin University and University of Wollongong campuses in GIFT City
  • University of Southampton campus in the Delhi NCR region
  • The same degree certificate as the home campus, at lower fees
  • All teaching in English, with no separate student visa for locals
Know More

Vietnam

Vietnam is one of the fastest-growing economies in Asia, and international campuses in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi let you study in English right next to that growth.

Quick facts

  • Among the lowest tuition and living costs in Southeast Asia
  • RMIT Vietnam and British University Vietnam run full campuses
  • Growing catalogue of English-taught and joint-degree programmes
  • One of Asia's fastest-growing economies for graduate hiring
  • Low crime rates and a welcoming international student community
Know More

Thailand

Thailand has quietly built a serious international education sector, offering English-taught degrees in one of the most affordable and welcoming countries in the region.

Quick facts

  • Chulalongkorn and Mahidol lead the national rankings
  • Some of the lowest tuition and living costs in Southeast Asia
  • Expanding range of English-taught international programmes
  • Recognised strengths in hospitality, tourism and Asian studies
  • University scholarships available for international applicants
Know More

Turkey

Sitting on the seam between Europe and Asia, Turkey offers European-recognised qualifications, a huge choice of universities and costs that stay comfortably below Western Europe.

Quick facts

  • 200+ universities across the country
  • Türkiye Bursları scholarships cover tuition, housing and a stipend
  • Bologna Process member, so degrees are recognised across Europe
  • English-taught options in medicine, engineering and business
  • Low tuition and living costs compared with Western Europe
Know More

Mauritius

Mauritius has positioned itself as a bilingual education hub in the Indian Ocean, combining internationally accredited programmes with an unusually easy route into professional work.

Quick facts

  • Bilingual English and French study environment
  • Overseas branch campuses awarding UK and Indian degrees
  • Young Professional Occupation Permit lets graduates stay and work
  • Affordable tuition and living costs for an accredited degree
  • Safe, politically stable and well connected to Africa and Asia
Know More
