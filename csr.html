Corporate Social Responsibility

We help students leave Bangladesh to study — and we work just as hard for the ones who stay.

Our commitment

Doing right by the community we come from

VFI was built in Dhaka, and most of our team grew up in the schools and neighbourhoods we now work with. Our CSR programme is deliberately small in scope and long in commitment — three areas, funded every year, measured honestly.

Access to education

Talent is spread evenly across Bangladesh; opportunity is not. We put money and counselling hours behind students whose results deserve a place but whose families cannot fund one.

Community support

Guidance is the cheapest thing we own and the most useful thing we can give. We take it into schools and colleges that have never had a careers adviser, free of charge and with nothing to sell.

Environmental responsibility

Our industry runs on air travel and paper. We cannot pretend otherwise, so we cut what we can in our own offices and fund planting and greening work in the city we operate from.

What we do

Six programmes we run every year

Each one has a named owner inside VFI, a budget line and a report at the end of the year. Nothing here depends on a good quarter.

Scholarships for underprivileged students

Every intake we cover application fees, test registrations and part of the first-year tuition gap for a small group of students selected on results and need alone. Recipients keep our counselling support at no cost until they land.

Free career-guidance camps in schools

Our counsellors spend one day a month in a college or higher-secondary school explaining what studying abroad actually costs, what it requires and when it is the wrong choice. No enrolment forms, no sales pitch.

Invite us to your school

Digital-literacy workshops

Short, practical sessions on the skills an application now demands — writing a clear email, filling an online portal, spotting a fake agent, keeping documents safe. Run for students and, on request, for their parents.

Request a workshop

Tree planting and campus greening

Each monsoon our staff plant with schools and colleges that give us the ground and agree to look after the saplings. We fund the plants, the guards and two years of watering, because planting without maintenance is theatre.

Disaster-relief contributions

Bangladesh floods and it storms, and when it does we release funds quickly rather than waiting for a campaign. Contributions go to registered relief organisations already working in the affected districts, matched against staff giving.

Volunteering time-off for staff

Everyone at VFI gets paid days each year to volunteer with a cause of their own choosing. Teaching, tutoring, clean-ups, blood drives — the time is theirs, the salary is ours, and no one has to make it up later.

Our CSR policy

Goodwill is not a policy. Ours is written down, approved by the board and reviewed once a year, so that our giving survives a change of management and a bad trading year alike. These are the four principles it rests on.

  • A defined annual budget. A fixed share of profit is committed to CSR at the start of each financial year and ring-fenced, so the programme is planned rather than improvised from whatever is left over.
  • An oversight committee. A small standing committee of directors and staff approves every project, signs off disbursements above a set threshold, and can stop any activity that drifts into marketing.
  • Transparent reporting. We publish what we spent, where it went and what it achieved once a year, including the projects that underperformed, and we keep receipts for every disbursement.
  • Partnering with registered non-profits. Funds are routed only through legally registered organisations with audited accounts. We never make cash payments to individuals and never attach our branding as a condition of support.
