How it works

Becoming a VFI partner

Five steps from first enquiry to your first submitted application.

01Apply and introduce your practice

Tell us where you operate, how many students you counsel in a year and which destinations you already handle. A regional manager calls you back to talk through the fit in both directions.

02Verification and agreement

We verify your business registration and references, then issue a partnership agreement covering territory, scope, conduct standards and commercial terms. Everything commercial is written down before you start.

03Onboarding and platform access

Your logins are created, your counsellors are added and we walk your team through search, shortlisting, document upload and submission on a live case rather than a slide deck.

04Training and certification

Counsellors complete the destination and documentation modules, sit an assessment and receive certification. After that they are invited to delegate webinars and regulation-update sessions throughout the year.

05Submit, track and settle

You counsel and submit; our admissions team reviews before dispatch and chases the university afterwards. You follow offers, deposits and visas on the platform, and settlement follows the terms in your agreement.

Good to know

Partner questions, answered

What agencies and sub-agents ask us before they sign.

Do I need an existing agency to partner with VFI?

A registered business and some counselling experience make onboarding far quicker, but we also work with independent counsellors who are building a practice. Talk to us about where you are and we will tell you honestly whether now is the right time.

Whose student is it once I submit through VFI?

Yours. You keep the relationship and the counselling. We process the application, support the difficult parts and stay behind you — we do not approach your students separately.

How is commission calculated and paid?

Rates vary by university and programme, so they are set out per institution in your agreement rather than as one blanket number. Each application shows what is expected against it, and payment is released after the university settles. We do not promise amounts we cannot control.

What support do I get on a difficult case?

Study gaps, backlogs, refusal history, sponsor documentation and borderline English scores are routine for our admissions and visa teams. Raise the case with your regional manager and you will get a considered view on where it can realistically go.
