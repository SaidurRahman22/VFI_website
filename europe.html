France

France is home to one of the most prestigious education systems in the world. Whatever subject you want to pursue, there are universities and grandes écoles that match your academic ambitions.

Quick facts

  • 64 Nobel Laureates and 15 Fields Medals
  • Post-study work visa up to 2 years after a master's
  • 3,500+ higher education institutions
  • 4 French universities in the world's top 100
  • Affordable tuition fees and living costs
Know More

Germany

Germany has become one of the most popular choices for international students, offering an unmatched learning ecosystem known for producing highly employable graduates.

Quick facts

  • 400,000+ international students
  • No tuition fees at most public universities
  • 18-month job-seeker visa after graduating
  • World leader in engineering and research
  • Blocked account of about €11,904 per year
Know More

Netherlands

The Netherlands pioneered English-taught education in continental Europe and remains one of the easiest places to study and work in English.

Quick facts

  • 9 universities in the world's top 200
  • 2,000+ English-taught programmes
  • One-year 'orientation year' search permit
  • Strong in business, engineering and design
  • Highly international student community
Know More

Sweden

Sweden's education system is built around independent thinking and group work, and its universities have deep links to global innovators.

Quick facts

  • 6 Swedish universities in the world's top 200
  • 1,000+ English-taught programmes
  • 12-month post-study residence permit
  • Innovation-led, research-driven teaching
  • Scholarships available for non-EU students
Know More

Austria

Austria combines low public-university fees with one of the highest standards of living in the world, centred on Vienna.

Quick facts

  • Low tuition fees at public universities
  • 12-month post-study residence permit
  • Programmes taught in English and German
  • Vienna ranked among the world's most liveable cities
  • Strong in music, medicine and engineering
Know More

Denmark

Denmark's problem-based learning approach puts you in real project work from your first semester, with close industry collaboration.

Quick facts

  • Highly ranked for research quality
  • 3-year establishment card after graduating
  • Wide range of English-taught master's degrees
  • Strong in design, sustainability and life sciences
  • Extensive scholarship and grant options
Know More

Finland

Finland is regularly named as having the world's best education system, with universities that put research and student wellbeing first.

Quick facts

  • Consistently top-ranked education system
  • 2-year post-study residence permit
  • Scholarships for non-EU students at most universities
  • World-class in technology, gaming and design
  • Very safe with excellent student services
Know More

Italy

Italy hosts the oldest university in the world and offers globally respected degrees at some of the lowest fees in Western Europe.

Quick facts

  • 90+ universities, including the world's oldest
  • Affordable tuition with income-based reductions
  • 12-month post-study residence permit
  • World leader in design, fashion and architecture
  • Regional scholarships for international students
Know More

Spain

Spain offers a growing range of English-taught degrees, top-ranked business schools and some of the lowest living costs in Western Europe.

Quick facts

  • Affordable tuition and living costs
  • 12-month job-search permit after graduating
  • Globally ranked business schools
  • Growing number of English-taught master's
  • Work up to 30 hours a week while studying
Know More

Switzerland

Switzerland is home to two of the world's top-ranked technical universities and the global centre of hospitality management education.

Quick facts

  • ETH Zurich and EPFL in the world's top 50
  • Global leader in hospitality management
  • 6-month job-search permit after graduating
  • Among the highest graduate salaries in Europe
  • Strong research funding and industry links
Know More

Hungary

Hungary offers internationally recognised degrees — particularly in medicine and engineering — at a fraction of Western European costs.

Quick facts

  • Very affordable tuition and living costs
  • Stipendium Hungaricum government scholarships
  • English-taught medical and engineering degrees
  • Degrees recognised across the EU
  • Budapest is a major student city
Know More

Poland

Poland has expanded its English-taught offer rapidly and remains one of the most affordable study destinations in the European Union.

Quick facts

  • Low tuition fees and living costs
  • 9-month post-study stay to look for work
  • Growing range of English-taught programmes
  • Strong in IT, engineering and medicine
  • Central location for travel across Europe
Know More
