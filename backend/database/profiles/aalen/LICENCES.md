## Logo: NOT used. Hero: used.

`aalen-logo.jpg` is downloaded and kept here but NOT written. The university's
own PNG logo was uploaded through the admin form
(`01M2SNB11DFP4B6KXNNWS9C7V3.png`) and serves correctly, so replacing it with a
Commons copy would be a downgrade. It stays as a licensed fallback.

`aalen-hero.jpg` IS written. The hero slot had the logo uploaded into it - an
easy mistake to make, because that field carried no size guidance at all - and
the banner cover-crops from the centre at roughly 5:1, so a wordmark rendered as
an unreadable blown-up pattern. Replaced with the CC0 campus photograph, which
is what this file was downloaded for.

## Not used, deliberately

The university's own campus photography (e.g. `csm_09_18.06.2025_14_*.jpg` on
hs-aalen.de) is copyrighted with no licence granted for third-party use. It was
rejected in review even though the URLs resolve. If a specific university photo
is wanted, ask the press office in writing first.

There is no square logo and no freely-licensed interior or laboratory photograph
anywhere - the Commons category holds 14 files and the usable ones are all
exterior aerials from one 2023 flight. For a university whose flagship field is
optics, a lab photo would be the single biggest improvement to this page, and
the only route to one is a written request to the press office.
