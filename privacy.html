Privacy Policy

What we collect when you apply through VFI, why we need it, who we share it with, and the control you keep over it.

Legal

Your information, and what we do with it

This wording is a starting point for a website template. Please have a qualified lawyer review and adapt it for your business before you publish it.
Last updated: 8 August 2026

1. What data we collect

We collect the details you give us: your name, contact details, date of birth, academic records, test scores, work history, passport and visa history, and the financial documents that support an application.

If you are only browsing the site, we collect basic technical data such as your browser type, approximate location and the pages you visit. We do not ask for information we do not need for your application.

2. How we use your data

We use your information to evaluate your profile, shortlist courses, prepare and submit applications, arrange test bookings, support visa applications and keep you updated on progress.

We also use it to answer your questions, to meet our own record-keeping obligations, and to see how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising unrelated to your study plans.

3. Sharing with universities and partners

To apply on your behalf we share your documents with the universities you have chosen, and where relevant with partners such as test centres, insurers, accommodation providers, banks and visa authorities.

We share only what a given organisation needs, and only for the destinations and courses you have agreed to. Once your data reaches a university or a government body, that organisation's own privacy rules apply to it alongside ours.

4. Cookies and analytics

This site uses a small number of cookies and similar browser storage to remember your preferences and to keep forms and saved items working between visits.

We may use analytics to see which pages are read most often so we can improve them. That tells us about traffic patterns rather than about you personally. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may then behave oddly.

5. How long we keep your data, and how we secure it

We keep your application file for as long as we are working with you and for a reasonable period afterwards, so we can answer follow-up questions and meet record-keeping requirements. After that we delete it, or remove the parts that identify you.

Access to your file is limited to the staff who need it, our systems are protected with passwords and access controls, and we ask our partners to protect your data too. No system anywhere can be promised to be perfectly secure, so we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your data.

6. Your rights over your data

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct anything that is wrong, or ask us to delete your file. You can also withdraw your consent to marketing messages at any time.

Write to dhaka@vfi-edu.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Please note that we may need to keep some records while an application is in progress, or where the law requires us to.

7. Children's and young people's data

Our services are intended for students aged 18 and over. Where an applicant is under 18, we ask that a parent or legal guardian gives consent and stays involved throughout the process.

If we learn that we hold a child's data without that consent, we will delete it. If you believe this has happened, please tell us straight away.

8. International transfers

Studying abroad means your information travels. Applying to a university in the UK, Canada, Australia, the USA, Ireland, New Zealand or elsewhere requires us to send your documents to organisations in those countries.

Data-protection rules in those countries may differ from those in Bangladesh. By asking us to apply on your behalf, you agree to those transfers for that purpose, and we send only what the application needs.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our services, our tools or our legal obligations change. The version published on this page is always the current one, and the date above tells you when it last changed.

If a change significantly affects how we handle your data, we will tell you by email or with a notice on the site. Otherwise, please check back here occasionally.

10. How to contact us

For any privacy question, correction request or complaint, write to us with your name and any reference number so we can find your file. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may raise the matter with the data-protection authority in your country.
