How it works

From first call to first cohort

Five steps, no surprises. Most institutions move from introduction to live representation within a few weeks.

01Discovery call and market fit

We walk through your programme portfolio, entry requirements, tuition bands and the student profile you want more of. If our market is not a strong match for what you offer, we will say so at this stage rather than after a wasted season.

02Agreement and compliance checks

A representation agreement setting out territories, programmes, commercial terms and conduct standards. We complete whatever due-diligence, agent-code or verification process your institution requires before anything goes live.

03Counsellor onboarding

Your team briefs ours on courses, scholarships, intakes and the things that quietly decide an offer. Your prospectus and criteria go into our counselling material so every conversation starts from accurate information.

04Sourcing, screening and submission

Candidates are counselled, shortlisted and screened against your requirements. Complete files are submitted through your preferred portal or channel, with a single point of contact for queries on any application.

05Offers, visas and enrolment reporting

We drive offer acceptance, deposits, visa preparation and pre-departure support — then report back on conversion at each stage so you can see where the intake is holding and where it is leaking.

Good to know

Questions from admissions teams

The things institutions usually want settled before signing.

Which markets can you recruit from?

Bangladesh is our home market and our strongest. We also work across Nepal, Sri Lanka and neighbouring South Asian markets through our regional offices. Territories are written into the agreement so there is no ambiguity about where we represent you.

How do you verify documents before submission?

Academic records are checked against issuing institutions, English test results against the awarding body's verification service, and financial evidence against the format your compliance team accepts. Anything we cannot verify does not get submitted under our name.

Can we send a delegate to your offices?

Yes, and we encourage it. We arrange the diary, invite shortlisted candidates, host interviews or spot-admission sessions and handle the local logistics. Give us a few weeks' notice and we will build the schedule around your travel dates.

What reporting do we receive?

Application, offer, acceptance, visa and enrolment numbers at whatever cadence suits your cycle, plus commentary on what is moving in the market. If your institution uses its own partner portal, we work inside it rather than asking you to adopt ours.
