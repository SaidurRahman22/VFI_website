How it works

From enquiry to opening day

Five stages. We would rather spend longer on stage two than have you open in the wrong place.

01Enquiry and first conversation

Tell us about your city, your background and what you want to build. We explain how the model works, what a franchisee is responsible for and what we handle centrally — before anyone talks about paperwork.

02Territory and feasibility review

We look at student demand in your area, existing coverage nearby and what the market can realistically support. You receive a full breakdown of setup requirements and running costs so you can plan with real numbers rather than optimistic ones.

03Agreement and office setup

The franchise agreement sets out territory, term, brand standards and commercial terms. In parallel we work with you on location, layout, branding, signage and the systems your counsellors will use.

04Hiring, training and launch

We help you recruit counsellors and train them at our offices on destinations, documentation and visa process. A launch campaign and opening seminar are planned with you so the office starts with enquiries, not an empty diary.

05Ongoing operations support

A regional manager reviews performance with you, the central admissions and visa desks back your team on complex files, and refresher training follows every significant change in university or immigration policy.

Good to know

Franchise questions, answered

The questions prospective franchisees ask us first.

Do I need experience in overseas education?

It helps, but it is not essential. What matters more is running a business well, hiring and keeping good people, and being willing to learn the sector properly. Counsellor expertise can be trained; commitment to the standard cannot.

What investment is involved?

It depends on your city, the size of the office and the team you start with, so we do not publish a single figure. After the feasibility review you receive a written breakdown of setup, fit-out, staffing and running costs for your specific location. We do not project earnings or promise returns — that would not be honest, and your results will depend on how you run the office.

Is my territory exclusive?

Your operating area is defined in the franchise agreement, along with the terms on which it is protected. We will tell you at the feasibility stage whether a city already has coverage rather than letting you find out afterwards.

How long does it take to open?

Typically a few months from agreement to launch, depending on how quickly premises and staff come together. The stage that most often sets the pace is hiring counsellors, so we start on that early rather than at the end.
