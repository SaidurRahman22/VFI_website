Good to know

Frequently asked questions

The questions students ask us most often, answered honestly.

When should I start my application?

Ideally 9–12 months before your intended intake. That leaves room for tests, applications, funding and the visa without rushing any of them.

What goes into an application package?

Academic transcripts, an English test score, a statement of purpose, letters of recommendation, your CV and proof of funds. We prepare each one with you.

Do you charge for counselling?

No. Profile evaluation and counselling are free, with no obligation to apply through us afterwards.

Can you help if my grades are average?

Yes. We build a shortlist around what your profile actually supports, and we are honest about which universities are realistic.

What happens if my visa is refused?

We review the refusal reasons with you, fix what caused them and reapply — or switch you to another intake or destination if that is the stronger option.
