Eligibility Criteria for Studying in Australia

Requirements vary between universities, but this is what Australian institutions typically ask for.

  • High school completed with a strong academic record
  • IELTS 6.0 – 6.5 overall
  • Or TOEFL iBT 79+ / PTE 58+ / Duolingo (selected universities)
  • Valid passport and proof of financial capacity
  • A completed bachelor's degree, 60% – 70% or equivalent
  • A related field of study is preferred
  • IELTS 6.5+ with no band below 6.0
  • SOP, CV and letters of recommendation

Top Student-Friendly Cities in Australia

Australia's student cities regularly top global liveability rankings — here's how the main four compare.

Melbourne, Victoria

Regularly ranked among the world's best student cities — excellent public transport, a huge arts scene, and two Group of Eight universities.

  • Living costAUD 24k – 30k / yr
  • Known forArts, finance, research

Sydney, New South Wales

Australia's largest job market and its most iconic city, home to the University of Sydney and UNSW.

  • Living costAUD 26k – 32k / yr
  • Known forFinance, tech, tourism

Brisbane, Queensland

Warm weather year-round, lower living costs than Sydney or Melbourne, and a fast-growing graduate job market.

  • Living costAUD 21k – 26k / yr
  • Known forEngineering, health, mining

Adelaide, South Australia

The most affordable of the major student cities, and classified as a regional area — which can mean extra post-study work time.

  • Living costAUD 19k – 24k / yr
  • Known forAffordability, regional benefits

FAQs

Can I mention PR plans in my visa application?

Yes. Under the Genuine Student framework, future PR aspirations are acceptable as long as studying remains your primary intent.

What is the Group of Eight?

Australia's eight leading research-intensive universities, recognised globally for research output and graduate outcomes.

How many years of post-study work can I get?

Master's graduates typically get 2–3 years. Some qualifications and regional locations extend this to as much as 5 years.

What is CRICOS?

The Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Students — the mandatory registration for any course offered to international students.

How many hours can I work while studying?

Up to 48 hours per fortnight while your course is in session, and unrestricted hours during scheduled breaks.

How long does the visa take?

Standard processing is usually one to three weeks once you have your CoE and a complete application.
